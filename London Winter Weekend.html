Fri 13 – Tue 17 November · West Hampstead base

London Winter Weekend

Three full days of markets, twinkling lights, a little wizardry and one very red windmill. Every day runs the same way: out all morning, home for rest and coffee, then out again for dinner and drinks.

❄Weather & what to wear
Fri–Sat outlook: dry, cold nights

The days

Getting around: tap a contactless card or phone on the tube; fares cap daily. Most days run on the Jubilee line from West Hampstead.
To book: Albert's Schloss (Sat), Sunday dinner, Old Compton Brasserie (Mon 17:45), Moulin Rouge tickets.
Hyde Park Winter Wonderland opens 19 Nov, two days after you leave.
